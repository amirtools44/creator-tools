How to use the YouTube Tag Generator

Good tags should accurately describe the video. Use this free YouTube tag generator as a brainstorming assistant: it creates phrase variations from your topic, but you should choose only the suggestions that genuinely match your content.

  1. Enter the main subject of your YouTube video.
  2. Select Generate Tags to create related tag ideas.
  3. Review and remove any suggestions that do not fit your video.
  4. Edit the tags if needed, then select Copy All Tags.
  5. Paste your chosen tags into the Tags field in YouTube Studio.

YouTube tag example

If your video is an easy chicken biryani recipe, relevant tag ideas could include:

chicken biryani, easy chicken biryani, chicken biryani recipe, homemade biryani, biryani cooking tutorial

This is an example, not a ranking list. Use only tags that accurately describe your actual video. Do not add a phrase just because it appears in a suggestion.

What makes a useful YouTube tag?

Choose specific phrases that closely match your video's subject, format, audience or intended search. Avoid unrelated tags and repeating phrases just to fill space.

Important note

This is a free, browser-based idea generator. It does not connect to YouTube or provide live keyword search volume, competition scores, ranking predictions or guaranteed results. According to YouTube's official guidance about tags, tags can help with common misspellings, but otherwise play a minimal role in helping people discover videos. Put more effort into an accurate title, an appealing thumbnail, a useful description and making a video viewers want to watch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are YouTube tags still useful?

Tags can help describe a video's topic and clarify common misspellings, but YouTube says they otherwise play a minimal role in discovery. Prioritize an accurate title, thumbnail and description.

Will these tags make my video go viral?

No tool can guarantee views or viral results. Use relevant tags as one small part of your overall YouTube optimization.

Can I edit the generated tags?

Yes. The tag list is editable. Remove irrelevant suggestions or add more accurate phrases before copying.

Does the generator use live YouTube data?

No. It creates basic keyword variations in your browser and does not retrieve live YouTube search or analytics data.
